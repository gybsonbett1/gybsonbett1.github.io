Built for how you actually use a hymnal

Whether you're leading song service, practicing at home, or looking up a hymn mid-conversation, everything's designed to be quick and out of your way.

Find a hymn by title, lyric, or number — across both the SDAH and NZK collections in a single search.

Lyrics and sheet music

Switch between full lyrics and scanned sheet music for SDAH hymns, right from the same screen.

A real pitch pipe

Favorites & playlists

Build as many playlists as you like, or just favorite the hymns you come back to most.

Free to use, respectful about it

SDA Hymnal and NZK is supported by occasional ads — never during the Sabbath, and never more than one every few minutes. There's also a free one-hour ad-free pass, once a week, if you'd rather have a stretch of uninterrupted reading.
