Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SDA Hymnal and NZK ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App. The App is a hymnal reference tool containing the SDAH and NZK song collections, and is designed to work fully offline. We built it with the goal of collecting as little information about you as possible.

Information we do not collect

The App does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location. Your favorites, playlists, recently viewed songs, and settings (font size, theme, default hymnal) are stored only on your device, in the App's local database. We do not have access to this information, and it is not transmitted anywhere by us.

Advertising and your Advertising ID

In plain terms: the App shows ads through Google AdMob, and to do that, AdMob uses your device's Advertising ID (and similar identifiers) to select and measure those ads.

The App is supported by advertising served through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners collect and use your device's Advertising ID, along with technical information about your device and app usage, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy. Where required by law (for example, under GDPR for users in the EEA/UK), the App presents a consent form before requesting personalized ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can review or withdraw consent through that form, or through your device's ad settings, at any time. For more detail on how Google handles this data, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

No ads during the Sabbath

As a design choice reflecting the App's audience, no advertisements are shown between Friday afternoon and Saturday evening (the Sabbath window), based on your device's local clock.

Data storage and retention

All hymn content, favorites, playlists, and settings are stored locally on your device using standard Android app storage. Uninstalling the App removes this data. We do not operate a server that stores your personal data, because the App does not send it to one.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

Your choices

You can limit ad personalization through your device's advertising settings, and you can clear all locally stored App data (favorites, playlists, settings) by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example to reflect changes in the App's features or in applicable law. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do.

Contact us
