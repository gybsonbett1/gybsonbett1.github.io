Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Marble Blast. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how data is handled while you enjoy our puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains the nature, scope, and purpose of the processing of personal and diagnostic data in connection with our mobile application.

2. Information We Process & Store

No account or sign-up is required to play Marble Blast. Your high scores, active grid state, streaks, combo counts, earned badges, unlocked skins, and sound/theme preferences are stored locally on your device only, via a local Room database and shared preferences — none of this leaves your device or is sent to us. • Device Identifiers: Standard non-identifying telemetry (e.g., device model, operating system version, crash diagnostics) may be collected to guarantee compatibility and smooth framerates.

3. Advertising & Google AdMob

Our app displays rewarded video advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may process non-sensitive identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy to serve contextually relevant or non-personalized advertisements. You can reset or opt out of personalized ad tracking anytime in your Android Device Settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID or Delete Advertising ID).

4. Audio Synthesis & Permissions

Our in-game sound effects are generated procedural-first using hardware AudioTrack and SoundPool synthesis. The game does not require or request microphone access, camera access, location permissions, contact lists, or external file storage permissions.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Marble Blast is designed as a family-friendly puzzle experience suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Union). If we learn that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child without parental consent, we will promptly delete that information.

6. Data Retention & Erasure

You maintain complete ownership and control of your gameplay records. Since everything is stored locally on your device, you can delete all your scores, streaks, and badges at any time by clearing the application data through Android App Settings (Settings > Apps > Marble Blast > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage).

7. Contact & Inquiries

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding our privacy practices or data rights, please contact us at kiplangatgybson1@gmail.com or by mail at 00100 Nairobi, Kenya.
