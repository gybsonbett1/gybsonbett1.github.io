Privacy Policy

Application: Zen Sudoku (com.zensudoku.android)
Effective Date: July 2026

1. Overview

Zen Sudoku is committed to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how user data is handled.

2. Data Collection & Local Storage

Zen Sudoku does not collect, harvest, or transmit personally identifiable information (PII) to external servers. All puzzle progress, statistics, theme preferences, and gameplay states are saved strictly locally on your device.

3. Advertising

Our app uses Google AdMob to serve non-intrusive advertisements. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), IP addresses, and app usage data to serve personalized or contextual ads and prevent fraud in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

4. Permissions

  • Internet & Network State: Used to load AdMob advertisements.
  • Notifications: Optional permission for daily puzzle reminders.
